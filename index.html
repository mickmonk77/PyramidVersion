Flips only the walls on the outer boundary of the maze, so their back/front face swaps — useful for wall objects whose front and back look different.

Floors — heights & tiles

Tiles use full size wherever the floor fits them and shrink only to fill gaps. Raise this above 1.05 to let tiles stretch to cover a little more, which cuts the tile count on big floors (stretched tiles are also thicker).
Floor tuning (test in-game)

Level-change markers

One marker at the exact centre of each level-change cell, at that floor's wall base height.

Pyramid slope

Pyramid mazes only. Adds a separate skin of sloped wall pieces over the top of the maze: each runs from the top edge of a floor's outer wall up to the top edge of the next floor's outer wall. The normal walls are not changed. Every piece is turned so the same face points outward on all four sides. If the pieces lean outward in-game, tick “Lean the other way”.

Floors exist for every level above the ground floor. Full-size tiles are used wherever they fit; around level-change cells they are resized (and raised to keep the surface level), leaving a one-cell hole for your stairs.

How to Use MazeGenerator.net

1

Visit MazeGenerator.net

Go to mazegenerator.net

2

Configure Your Maze

  • Shape: Choose "Orthogonal" (square grid)
  • Size: Set your desired width and height (e.g., 20x20)
  • Algorithm: Any algorithm works
  • Cell size: Use default (20px works well)
  • Wall thickness: 1px recommended
3

Generate & Download

  • Click "Generate" to create your maze
  • Click "Download" and save as PNG
  • Tip: The filename should include dimensions (e.g., "20x20 maze.png")
4

Import to This Tool

  • Drag & drop the PNG file into the upload area
  • Dimensions will auto-detect from filename
  • Adjust settings if needed
  • Generate your DayZ JSON!

Quick Tips

  • For DayZ: Keep mazes under 50x50 for performance
  • Best results: Use black walls on white background
  • File naming: Include dimensions like "30x40 maze.png"
  • Complex mazes: May require threshold adjustment in this tool
